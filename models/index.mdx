varg provides access to 120+ AI models from multiple providers through a single API. 1 credit = $0.01. The live list with prices is at [`GET https://api.varg.ai/v2/models`](https://api.varg.ai/v2/models). For AI agents, every model also has a Markdown spec with its inputs, limits and the exact call — see [Model specs for agents](/models/agent-specs).
